# Stride — a little further

A personal Android couch-to-5K coach with a 12-week plan, spoken workout cues, progress tracking, and optional GPS routes.

**[Download Stride for Android](https://github.com/codescribblr/stride-downloads/releases/latest/download/Stride.apk)**

[Latest release and installation files](https://github.com/codescribblr/stride-downloads/releases/latest) · [Full installation and restore instructions](INSTALL.txt)

No GitHub account or sign-in is required. Android 8.0 or later is required.

1. Open the download link on your Android device.
2. Open the downloaded **Stride.apk**. If prompted, allow installs from your browser or Files, then tap **Install** or **Update**.
3. Open Stride, allow notifications, and choose your reminder time and coach voice in Settings.

For updates, install over the existing app. **Do not uninstall first.** Bookmark this page for future versions; updates are manual.

The installer contains no personal workout history. To move your progress, save a full backup in Stride Settings, copy it off your old phone, then restore it in Settings on the new phone. Devices do not automatically sync.

This repository provides installers and instructions only. The app is a signed personal debug build distributed outside the Play Store. Workout records and GPS routes remain on your device unless you export them; online street maps load when viewed.
